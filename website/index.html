Three Privacy Modes

Choose the level of privacy you want for each browsing session.

MODE 01

Normal Mode

Fast, everyday browsing with automatic ad blocking and tracker removal. Browsing history is turned off by default so your device stays completely clean.

Private Mode

Extra isolation for sensitive sites. Cookies, cache, and site data are deleted the moment you close the tab, leaving zero trace on your phone.

MODE 03

Tor Mode

Maximum privacy. Bounces your connection through encrypted Tor relays to hide your real identity and location from websites and network providers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Simple, straightforward answers about privacy, downloads, and how the app works.

What is Mr Nobody?

Mr Nobody is a free, open source Android browser designed to protect your privacy. It comes with a built in research tool that finds facts with sources, blocks ads and tracking, and gives you a reliable download manager.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No. There are no accounts, signups, passwords, or subscriptions. You download the app, open it, and start browsing immediately.

Does Mr Nobody track my searches or browsing?

No. Browsing history is turned off by default, tracking parameters are stripped from links, and known advertising networks are blocked right on your device. We do not collect or sell your data.

How does the smart research tool work?

Instead of guessing or making up plausible sounding answers, Mr Nobody reads real web pages, strips out the ads and junk, and extracts the key facts with direct links so you can verify the information yourself.

What makes downloads different in Mr Nobody?

Most mobile browsers fail if your internet connection drops for a second. Mr Nobody download engine saves your exact byte progress so you can pause, resume, and finish large downloads without restarting from zero.

Is Mr Nobody really 100% free?

Yes. Mr Nobody is open source software licensed under the MIT License. Anyone can inspect the code, verify its privacy, and use it freely without paywalls or hidden fees.
